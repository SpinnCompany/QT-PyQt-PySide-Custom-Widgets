# Contributor License Agreement (DRAFT)

> **⚠️ DRAFT — must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before use.**
> This is a starting point modeled on common individual CLAs (Apache ICLA
> structure) with an added **relicensing/sublicensing grant** required for this
> project's dual-licensing (free LGPL core + commercial Pro). It is **not legal
> advice**. Names, entity, and jurisdiction are placeholders.
>
> Two agreements are provided: an **Individual CLA (ICLA)** for people
> contributing on their own behalf, and a short **Corporate CLA (CCLA)** note for
> contributions made on behalf of an employer.

## Corporate CLA (CCLA) — short form note

When a contributor is acting within the scope of employment, or the employer owns
the IP, the **employer** should execute a Corporate CLA that:
- makes the same §2 copyright and §3 patent grants (including the
  sublicense/relicense rights) on behalf of the corporation, and
- lists the authorized employees ("Schedule A") permitted to contribute, kept
  up to date by a designated corporate signatory.

A full CCLA should be drafted by counsel alongside the ICLA.
